About

Builder, tinkerer, signal chaser.

I'm Joseph Burt, based in the DFW area. I break things to understand them, build things to prove I can, and share what I learn.

Most of what I do sits at the intersection of hardware, software, and curiosity. This site is the homepage for that work — projects, experiments, and whatever I'm on this week.
